# 2-minute video

0:00 to 0:15. The power line page, calm air. "Before a drone inspects a live line in the wind,
someone has to sign it off. Falcon makes that an agent's job."

0:15 to 0:25. Press "Clear for flight". "One brief: tomorrow's inspection, 3 to 5 m/s toward the
line, gusty."

0:25 to 0:45. Identify and Predict light up. Show the hover runs, then the code the agent wrote and
its prediction. "It measures drag from the accelerometer, then writes code to predict where it runs
out of tilt. It commits to that number before it tests."

0:45 to 1:10. Expand. The envelope chart fills in and the crosses appear at the edge. If the agent
tried to skip ahead, show the build-up refusal. "Step by step, never more than 2 m/s past what it
has cleared. The drone departs earlier than drag alone predicts, and the agent explains why: rotor
drag pitches the vehicle back. We checked by removing that torque, and the edge moves up."

1:10 to 1:30. The red rejection, then the green stamp. "Its first report misquoted a number. The
checker caught it against the flight log, and it fixed it. Every number you see links to a flight."

1:30 to 1:50. Show live: wind at the limit, a gust, three drones. "Then it flies the limit live.
The learner holds within centimetres, because its learned force can only remove energy."

1:50 to 2:00. "Falcon. Autonomy that can't add energy and can't make up a number."
